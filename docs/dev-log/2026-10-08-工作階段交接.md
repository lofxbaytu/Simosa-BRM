## CI 修正(第十五輪後;commit bc27b04 的 Windows .NET 工作失敗)

- 失敗:`MultiClientRangeTests.TwoClientsGetTheirOwnRangeAndControlsAreForwarded`,客戶端 b 的 `CloseAsync` 擲 `WebSocketException: The remote party closed the WebSocket connection without completing the close handshake`(僅 Windows;Linux/本機通過)。
